Design review · v1
Member app & admin portal

Subaidar Hasnain Foundation

A welfare application built around one promise: a family in difficulty can ask for help in under a minute, attach the bill or medical report that proves the need, and then watch exactly where their request sits. Every screen below is rendered from the app's own stylesheet — what you see is the built product, not a mockup of it.

4 funds Monthly · Accidental · Grocery · Electricity 4 stages Requested → Review → Accepted → Transferred Stack Next.js on Vercel · Supabase
01 — Foundations

Palette & surface

Emerald carries the brand; gold marks anything awaiting a person's decision. Each fund gets its own gradient so members recognise a card before they read it. Behind everything sits a light geometric pattern at low opacity — texture without noise.

06 — How it is built

Behind the screens

One Next.js app on Vercel serves the member PWA, the admin portal and the APIs. Supabase holds the database, the auth and the private document storage.

InstallableThe member app is a PWA — “Add to Home Screen” gives it an icon, a splash screen and no browser chrome.
Private by defaultCNICs, reports and bills live in a private bucket. Members see only their own files; admins open any, through a link that expires in ten minutes.
Enforced in the databaseRow-level security means a member cannot read another member's application even if the API were bypassed entirely.
Audited automaticallyEvery status change writes a timeline row from a Postgres trigger — the history cannot drift from the record.
Funds are dataThe four funds are rows, not code. Adding a fifth is an insert, with its own gradient, icon and limits.
ReferencesEach application gets a human reference (SHF-26-01042) the office and the member can both quote on the phone.
